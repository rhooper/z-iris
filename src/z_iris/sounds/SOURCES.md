# Sound sources

Bundled recordings from Wikimedia Commons that are in the public domain or CC0
(no rights reserved). Each was trimmed to at most 3 s around the sound, made
mono 44.1 kHz and normalized to the same loudness as the other tones. The
synthesized tones (dx-*, moog-a, sos and the sox tones) are generated by the
scripts in scripts/claude/ and need no attribution.
